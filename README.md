# 传承中文 — Self-Study System
### *Modern Chinese for Heritage Beginners: Stories About Us* — Liu, Ji, Wu & Liang (Routledge, 2023)

A complete, do-it-every-day study system built **from the book itself**: a day-by-day lesson plan plus
Anki spaced-repetition decks covering the book's vocabulary, grammar patterns, and character radicals.

**Tuned for:** deep long-term mastery · ~45 minutes/day · every day · flexible (no deadline) pace.

---

## Start here

1. **Read [`lesson-plan.md`](lesson-plan.md)** — the day-by-day schedule (≈11 weeks). It tells you
   exactly what to read and study each session.
2. **Set up Anki using [`spaced-repetition-guide.md`](spaced-repetition-guide.md)** — ~20 minutes,
   one time. This is where the retention actually happens.
3. **Begin Day 1** (Foundation: pinyin + characters). Do your 45 minutes. Repeat daily.

---

## What's in here

| File / folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| **`lesson-plan.md`** | The core day-by-day plan: 79 days across Foundation + 10 lessons, with reading pages, objectives, grammar, daily recall tasks, and built-in review days. |
| **`spaced-repetition-guide.md`** | Step-by-step Anki setup (note types, import, deck options, FSRS) and your daily review loop. |
| **`anki/vocabulary_all.tsv`** | All **437** vocabulary cards (simplified · traditional · pinyin · part-of-speech · English), tagged by lesson and part. |
| **`anki/grammar_patterns_all.tsv`** | All **85** grammar/sentence-pattern cards, each with a usage note and a real example from the book. |
| **`anki/传承中文.apkg`** | **Ready-to-import** deck — double-click and all cards + card templates appear (no manual setup). |
| **`anki/foundation_pinyin.tsv`** | **38** Introduction Q/A cards (initials, finals, tones, tone-change & spelling rules), tagged by day for Days 1–4. |
| **`anki/foundation_radicals.tsv`** | **25** character-radical cards (radical · pinyin · meaning · example characters). |
| **`anki/by-lesson/`** | The same vocab + grammar split into per-lesson files (`L01_*` … `L09_*`) for drip-feeding one lesson at a time. |

All decks are **tab-separated files with Anki header lines**, so importing auto-maps the columns and
tags. The guide explains the two-card (recognition + production) note type that makes each word stick.

---

## How the daily 45 minutes is spent

```
12–15 min  Anki reviews (clear everything due) — always first
20–25 min  New material from the book (read + study vocab/grammar)
 5–10 min  Active output (say/write 2–3 sentences) + release the day's new cards
```

Reviews come first and never get skipped — that's the whole trick. See the plan and guide for details.

## The book at a glance

5 units × 2 lessons = **10 lessons**, each with a **dialogue (对话)** and an **essay (短文)**, moving
from the individual outward to family, language, education, immigration, and community:

1. 我和家庭 — Me & Family (我的名字 · 我是华裔美国人)
2. 家庭语言 — Family Language (我家的方言 · 普通话和方言)
3. 教育 — Education (家庭教育 · 中文教育)
4. 移民故事 — Immigration Stories (移民故事 · 中国版图)
5. 社区和社会 — Community & Society (中国城 · 中国城的未来)

## Coverage & how to complete it

The plan and decks are **complete for the Foundation section and Lessons 1–8**, plus **Lesson 9's
dialogue**. Lesson 9's essay and all of Lesson 10 fell beyond the portion of the source PDF that could
be extracted, and are marked as placeholders in the plan. To finish them, re-share pages **182–198**
(and L9's essay) and those days + cards will be filled in identically to the rest. Details in the
"Finishing Lessons 9–10" section of `lesson-plan.md`.
