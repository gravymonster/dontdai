# 传承中文 — Vocabulary & Phrases Track (day-by-day)
### A focused plan to memorize *every* word and phrase in the book via spaced repetition

This is a **vocabulary-first** companion to `lesson-plan.md`. Where the lesson plan walks you
through reading each dialogue and essay, this track exists for one job: **drive every vocabulary
word and grammar phrase from the book into long-term memory** as fast as is sustainable, using Anki.

**Profile:** ~45 min/day · deep mastery · ~20 new words/day (heritage learners already know many,
so this is brisk but realistic). Each word becomes **2 Anki cards** (see→meaning *and* meaning→write),
and each lesson's grammar **phrases** are woven in alongside its words.

> **Coverage:** This schedule covers the **437 words + 85 phrases** extracted so far — all of
> Lessons 1–8 plus Lesson 9's dialogue. Lesson 9's essay, Lesson 10, and the book's back glossary
> (pp. 199–217) are pending source text; see [Completing coverage](#completing-coverage). They'll
> append to the end of this schedule when added.

---

## How to run it in Anki

1. In Anki, open **`传承中文` → gear ⚙️ on `2 Vocabulary` → Options → New cards/day = `40`** (= 20 words × 2 cards).
2. **`3 Grammar` → New cards/day = `4`** (the phrases).
3. Cards are already ordered by lesson, so studying daily introduces them in exactly the order below.
4. Keep **FSRS on @ 0.90** and **reviews-first** (see `spaced-repetition-guide.md`).
5. The day-by-day table is your **map** — Anki does the scheduling; this tells you where you are and when each lesson wraps.

**Daily 45 min:** clear all *due* reviews first (~25–30 min once it ramps), then today's new words
(~10 min), then 5 min writing 2–3 of the new words into sentences out loud.
