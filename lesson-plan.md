# 传承中文 — Day-by-Day Lesson Plan
### *Modern Chinese for Heritage Beginners: Stories About Us* (Liu, Ji, Wu & Liang, Routledge 2023)

A structured, every-day plan built for **deep mastery** at **~45 minutes/day**, with Anki spaced
repetition woven through every session. Pace is **flexible** — the plan optimizes for retention and
sustainability, not speed. If a day feels heavy, split it across two days; the schedule will wait.

> **Scope note (read once):** This plan was generated by ingesting the book's actual text. The
> Foundation section and **Lessons 1–8 are complete**, and **Lesson 9's dialogue** is included.
> Lesson 9's essay and all of Lesson 10 were beyond the portion of the PDF that could be extracted,
> so those days are scaffolded as placeholders — see [Finishing L9–L10](#finishing-lessons-9-10).
> Re-share those pages and I'll drop the vocab/cards straight in.

## How each 45-minute session is built

Do these **in this order** every day. Reviews first — they are the part that actually builds
long-term memory.

| Block | Time | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Anki reviews** | 12–15 min | Clear all *due* cards in your Anki deck. Never skip. Be honest with Again/Hard/Good/Easy. |
| **2. New material** | 20–25 min | Read the day's section of the book; study the new vocabulary and/or grammar pattern(s). |
| **3. Active output** | 5–10 min | Say or write 2–3 sentences using today's new words/patterns. Then **unsuspend** the day's new cards in Anki so they enter rotation tomorrow. |

**Golden rules for deep mastery**
1. **Reviews are non-negotiable.** If you only have 15 minutes one day, do reviews and skip new cards.
2. **Cap new cards at ~12–15/day.** The plan respects this. More than that and reviews snowball.
3. **Read aloud.** Heritage learners already have an ear for the language — use it. Pronounce every new word.
4. **Produce, don't just recognize.** The 5–10 min output block is where passive knowledge becomes active.
5. **The review days are the plan, not a break.** Don't skip them to "make progress."

See **`spaced-repetition-guide.md`** for the exact Anki setup (note type, intervals, daily limits).

---

## The weekly rhythm

```
Mon–Sat : new material (read + study + produce + add cards)
Every 6th day of a lesson : Lesson Consolidation (no new cards — review + re-read aloud)
End of each Unit : Unit Review day (cumulative, no new cards)
```

You study **every day**. "Review days" still take 45 minutes — they're just spent reviewing and
re-reading instead of adding new cards. That cadence is what makes things stick.

# Phase 0 — Foundation (基础篇) · Days 1–7
**Pages 1–22.** The book opens with a Pinyin chapter (拼音篇) and a Character chapter (汉字篇:
strokes 笔画, stroke order 笔顺, and 25 core radicals 部首 illustrated with the 165 characters that
recur in Unit 1). The authors recommend drilling this **before** Lesson 1 — do not skip it even if
you can already speak. It makes every later character "click" instead of memorized blind.

> **Anki:** The Foundation cards are already in the deck. Study the **`1 Foundation (Pinyin & Rules)`**
> subdeck during Days 1–4 (38 cards covering initials, finals, tones, tone-change & spelling rules —
> tagged `day::01`…`day::04`), and the **`1 Foundation (Radicals)`** subdeck on Days 5–7 (25 cards).
> Set each subdeck to ~10 new cards/day when you reach it.

| Day | Focus | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Pinyin: initials 声母 | Read 拼音篇 (p.1). Drill all initials aloud. Do the book's 拼音练习. Study Anki `day::01` cards (the 21 initials, aspiration, x/s/sh). |
| **2** | Pinyin: finals 韵母 + tones | Drill finals and the four tones + neutral tone. Practice minimal pairs (mā/má/mǎ/mà). Study Anki `day::02` cards. |
| **3** | Pinyin: combination rules | Spelling rules (j/q/x + ü→u, w/y, tone placement, 不/一 tone changes). Finish 拼音练习. Study Anki `day::03` cards. |
| **4** | Strokes 笔画 & stroke order 笔顺 | Read 汉字笔画和笔顺 (p.7). Learn the 8 basic strokes and the rules of stroke order. Hand-write 5 characters following correct order. |
| **5** | Radicals 部首 (set 1) | Radicals 1–13 (人/女/言/辶/口/日/心/木/糸/水/手/刀/艹). For each, note meaning + 2 example characters. Add cards. |
| **6** | Radicals 部首 (set 2) | Radicals 14–25 (犭/土/广/阝/囗/足/寸/走/夕/月/父/竹). Add cards. |
| **7** | **Foundation review** | Re-drill any shaky pinyin. Quiz yourself on all 25 radicals (meaning + an example). Full Anki review. No new cards. |

**Foundation goals:** read any pinyin syllable with correct tone; write characters in correct stroke
order; recognize the 25 radicals and predict a character's rough meaning category from its radical.

# Per-lesson day structure (the template)

Every lesson runs on the **same 6-day rhythm**. Each lesson below fills in the specifics.

| Day | Phase | Typical content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dialogue · part 1** | Listen to/read the 对话; study first half of dialogue vocab; add cards |
| 2 | **Dialogue · part 2** | Finish dialogue vocab; study dialogue grammar patterns; do dialogue exercises |
| 3 | **Essay · part 1** | Read the 短文; study first half of essay vocab; add cards |
| 4 | **Essay · part 2** | Finish essay vocab; study essay grammar patterns |
| 5 | **Integration** | Re-read both texts aloud; do the lesson's communicative task (interview/survey/presentation) and a short writing |
| 6 | **Consolidation** | No new cards. Full Anki review; re-read dialogue + essay; self-quiz on objectives |

> **Adding cards:** On days 1–4 you *unsuspend* that lesson's cards in chunks. Import the whole
> lesson at once (`anki/by-lesson/L0X_vocabulary.tsv` + `L0X_grammar.tsv`) and suspend them, then
> unsuspend the day's batch — or just import each day's batch. The guide explains both.
