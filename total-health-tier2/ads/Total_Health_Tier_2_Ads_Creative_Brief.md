# Total Health Tier 2 — static ad creative brief

## Status

Three 4:5 review boards are included with this package. They are manually rendered, brand-matched fallback concepts. Goose Ads generation was attempted through the required skill workflow, but the connector returned no result on repeated skill, identity, and template-catalog calls. Do not represent the boards as Goose-generated outputs.

## Default Goose generation plan

- Template choice: **Surprise Me** (user did not answer the timed template prompt).
- Styling: **Keep original template styling** (required documented default when no preference is provided).
- Format: three static 4:5 creatives, one version per angle.
- Engine: `gpt_image_2`, medium quality.
- Brand: Total Health; use the official public site as the visual reference.
- Required final checks: Meta ad policy checker, ad-to-landing-page auditor, spelling, logo fidelity, legibility at mobile size, and no unsupported health or outcome claim.

## Angle 1 — Record clarity

**Headline:** When cancer records are scattered, clarity matters.

**Support:** A specialist-reviewed report designed for the patient and care team.

**Visual:** Black Total Health field, orbital data motif, one white decision-summary report.

**Why:** Leads with an observable operational problem and the artifact, without implying a diagnosis, criticizing a prior clinician, or promising a treatment change.

## Angle 2 — Three questions

**Headline:** One report built around the questions that matter most.

**Prompts:** What does the record support? What is worth discussing next? Could another test inform the decision?

**Visual:** Large “03,” compact question list, restrained black/sage system.

**Why:** Makes the bounded review concrete and consistent with the intake and report information architecture.

## Angle 3 — Care-team support

**Headline:** Built to support the care team—not replace it.

**Support:** One version; two reading layers.

**Visual:** The patient summary and clinical-detail layers of one report shown side by side.

**Why:** Reinforces treating-clinician primacy and connects the ad directly to the landing page and the product’s core deliverable.

## Safety and policy guardrails

- Do not use “Do you have cancer?” or any copy that asserts or implies a viewer’s health condition.
- Do not claim better outcomes, guaranteed plan changes, faster treatment, diagnosis correction, or specific test eligibility.
- Do not say Total Health finds hereditary cancer or that direct-to-consumer testing rules out inherited risk.
- Do not disparage the treating team or imply that prior clinicians missed something.
- Do not place third-party health-data retargeting, session replay, or ad pixels on authenticated or PHI-bearing pages.
- Link only to the public, no-PHI landing page; move to authenticated intake through a deliberate transition.
- Keep “prototype,” availability, emergency-care, and clinician-review boundaries visible during the pre-launch phase.
