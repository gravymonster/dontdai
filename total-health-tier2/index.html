THE PRODUCT

Your records tell a story. We make it easier to see.

Cancer information can sit across hospitals, labs, pathology reports, imaging summaries, molecular tests, and treatment notes. Total Health brings the relevant pieces together, traces every conclusion to its source, and helps you prepare for the next conversation with your treating team.

WHAT IT IS

A structured second look.

  • Record collection with your permission
  • Answers to your three priority questions
  • Clinician-reviewed findings and testing considerations
  • A guided discussion and provider handoff

WHAT IT ISN'T

A replacement for your oncologist.

  • Not emergency or inpatient care
  • Not a promise that your plan will change
  • Not raw imaging or pathology review in this pilot
  • Not an automated treatment decision

A NARROW V0

Designed for a specific moment in care.

This pilot may fit when you…

  • are an adult with an established cancer diagnosis
  • have an existing oncologist or treating clinician
  • are stable and receiving outpatient care
  • want clarity on a current plan, test, or decision
  • can authorize records from U.S. facilities

Another service is needed when…

  • you have an emergency or worsening symptoms
  • you are hospitalized or need crisis support
  • the question requires a physical examination
  • raw scans or pathology slides must be re-read
  • your treatment timeline cannot safely wait

QUESTIONS

What patients usually want to know.

Will you collect my records?

Yes. After you authorize us, a coordinator requests the relevant records from the facilities you name. You may upload records you already have, but upload is optional.

How long does the review take?

The record-gathering time depends on your providers. The proposed pilot target is 7–10 business days for the report after a clinician confirms that the record set is complete. Final terms are pending.

Does this replace my oncologist?

No. Your treating clinician remains responsible for diagnosis, orders, and treatment. The report is designed to support a better-informed conversation with that team.

What if my treatment is starting soon?

Tell us during the fit check. We will only accept the case if the clinical team can meet the decision timeline. Do not delay treatment while waiting for this review unless your treating clinician advises you to.

Will my doctor receive the report?

The proposed service sends the same versioned report to you and the clinician you nominate through verified secure channels, subject to your authorization and the final service policy.
