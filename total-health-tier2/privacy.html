PROTOTYPE PRIVACY

This demonstration does not collect or transmit patient information.

The fit-check and patient-flow pages run entirely in your browser memory. They have no form endpoint, analytics SDK, advertising pixel, session replay, account, database, or file upload. Choices are cleared when the page is refreshed or closed.

Do not enter real patient information

The prototype uses predefined choices and sample names. It is not approved for protected health information, diagnosis details, clinician identities, dates of birth, records, or contact information.

What production requires

A real service needs an approved privacy and security architecture, documented legal role, required agreements, access controls, audit logs, encryption, retention policy, incident response, state-specific clinical coverage, and approved patient consent/records-release documents.

Advertising and analytics boundary

The proposed production design separates the public marketing site from authenticated health intake. Health-data retargeting and third-party tracking on protected pages are excluded unless a formal legal/security review approves a specific configuration.
